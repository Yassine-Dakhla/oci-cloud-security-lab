Finding

Strict-Transport-Security missing

Medium
Why it matters

Without HSTS, a browser may attempt an insecure HTTP connection before being redirected to HTTPS.

Recommended action

After confirming HTTPS works correctly across the site, configure an appropriate Strict-Transport-Security response header.
