Designed for safe checks

Useful visibility without turning ForgeSec into an attack proxy.

The public scanner intentionally avoids exploit attempts, brute force, directory discovery and mass port scanning.

  • 01
    Private networks blocked

    Targets resolving to localhost, private, link-local, reserved or other non-public addresses are rejected.

  • 02
    DNS result pinned

    The outbound connection uses an already-validated public address to reduce DNS-rebinding and SSRF risk.

  • 03
    Rate limited

    Basic per-client limits and short connection/read timeouts reduce abuse and resource exhaustion.
