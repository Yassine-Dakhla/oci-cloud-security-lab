Traffic strategy

Useful content first. Search traffic second.

ForgeSec's resources will be written to solve real questions people have about website security. Search optimization will help people discover those answers, but the content should still be worth reading if Google did not exist.

Stay in one niche

Focus on website security, deployment hygiene, TLS, headers, DNS and monitoring instead of chasing every cybersecurity topic.

Connect content to tools

A guide about HSTS should naturally lead to the Header Analyzer; a TLS guide should lead to the TLS Inspector.

Keep product pages clean

If advertising is introduced later, it belongs mainly on informational resource pages — not inside the core scanner workflow.

Learn, then verify

Read the explanation. Check the configuration.

ForgeSec is designed so educational content and practical tools strengthen each other.
